What we make

Cheddar

A cool camera built for your creative kid. Kids snap photos with their voice, then turn them into stories, silly voices, scavenger hunts and games, all made from their own pictures, all on the phone. Simple enough for a three-year-old. No ads, no tracking, and photos stay on the device.

Get it on Google Play

Dailies

Film your day. Every photo keeps the three seconds before it, sound and all. Complete the day and they play back as one short film.

Visit dailies.cam

Posties

An audio postcard. Pick one photo, talk over it for twenty seconds, and send it to one person you love. It lands in Messages, where they already are. Coming to iPhone.

Join the waitlist

Emoji Time

Pick three emojis and hear a short, silly story about them, read out loud, for a grown-up and a kid to listen to together. Coming soon.
